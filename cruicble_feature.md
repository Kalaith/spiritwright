# Spiritwright: Shifting Crucible, Boss Royale

## Goal

Create a boss survival gauntlet that tests the player's ability to construct a versatile six-golem team capable of defeating multiple challenging encounters without rebuilding.

The mode should extend Spiritwright's collection and golem-engineering gameplay rather than introduce an unrelated progression system.

## Core rules

- Players select up to six golems from their persistent roster before entering.
- A run consists of six boss encounters followed by a final encounter.
- The selected spirits, vessels, equipment and formations are locked throughout the run.
- Losing any encounter immediately ends the run.
- Failed runs restart from the first encounter.
- Health, statuses and defeated units reset between successful encounters for the initial implementation.
- Players retain their permanent collection and campaign progression after defeat.
- Enemy levels should be normalised or scaled to the chosen Crucible difficulty.
- Record the furthest encounter reached, completed runs and winning team configurations.

## Encounter design

- Use existing bosses and wild spirit combinations where possible.
- Each encounter should test a different aspect of team composition, such as burst damage, sustain, defensive protection, retaliation or status effects.
- Do not rely solely on increasing enemy health or damage.
- Ensure a variety of team compositions can complete the full gauntlet.
- Provide scouting information about the upcoming sequence before the run begins.

## Replayability

- Classic Royale: fixed boss sequence.
- Shifting Royale: seeded boss order and encounter combinations.
- Support additional difficulty tiers after the initial mode is balanced.
- A weekly fixed seed may be introduced later.

## Rewards

- Provide a completion record and progression milestones.
- Prioritise cosmetic rewards, titles, codex entries and vessel finishes.
- Avoid mandatory power rewards that make later attempts trivially easier.

## Out of scope for v1

- Temporary boons.
- Forge stops or equipment upgrades.
- Changing the team between encounters.
- Permanent spirit death.
- New combat mechanics solely for this mode.
- Randomised enemy behaviour that violates deterministic combat.

## Design success criteria

The player should lose, examine which encounter defeated their team, change their overall composition, and want to attempt the full gauntlet again.

The challenge is not solving each boss individually. It is designing one team capable of defeating every boss.

Reward The Boss Royale spirit

Vesper, the Crucible Spirit
Proposed unique reward
A spirit forged from the echoes of defeated champions. Unlike the elemental specialists, Vesper thrives in unusual vessel configurations.
Trait: Adaptation
Gains a modest bonus based on its vessel's frame, encouraging experimentation rather than being universally stronger than other spirits.
Temperament: Restless
Prefers changing equipment between battles.

One possible twist: Vesper doesn't have a preferred material. Instead, it gains a different minor effect depending on which material it inhabits.
